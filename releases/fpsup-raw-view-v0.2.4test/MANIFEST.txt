Against v0.2.3test:
  - DNG ColorMatrix1/2 at Saturation +0.2 (projects/rawview/build/rv_dngcm.py):
    C_k = diag(O.W) . inv(M_k) . XYZ->709 . Bradford(W -> D65), O = the factory
    OFF constant for illuminant W (StdA / D65), M_k = the LCD's matrix. Before,
    +0.2 wrote Sigma's non-OFF constants N, and the same AsShotNeutral that the
    OFF tags decode as 6503 K decoded as 7614 K under N: Resolve's white moved
    about 1100 K when Saturation was raised. Now every level decodes the same
    white (2857 K / 6503 K) and the decoded colour equals the LCD's M_k.
    The dcraw-style row-normalised check could not see it (normalising IS
    white -> white); the build now asserts the decoded white, and the old
    construction is kept as a control that must fail. Found by Luka
    (fp-view-pack v0.2 / rv-chartfit.patch, 2026-10-03).
  - The matrix itself is unchanged (Sigma's calibration); Saturation 0 still
    writes the factory OFF bytes.
  - rawview payload: 126 bytes differ from a v0.2.3test rebuild, all inside the
    +0.2 level of the ColorMatrix table. fpSup.BIN as a whole differs from the
    published v0.2.3test because the shared loader moved since (FPSUPUI/,
    "Faster start for every card").
