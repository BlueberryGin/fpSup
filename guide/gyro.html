fpSup-Gyro

While you record CinemaDNG, it writes the two files Gyroflow needs into the clip's own folder: a .gcsv motion log and a .json lens profile. Current version v1.14.1test. It reduces GCSV formatting work; reported audio drift has not yet been checked on a camera. Test with expendable footage.

錄 CinemaDNG 的同時,它會把 Gyroflow 要的兩個檔案寫進片子自己的資料夾: .gcsv 運動記錄和 .json 鏡頭 profile。 目前版本 v1.14.1test。GCSV 轉換工作量已減少，回報的音訊漂移 仍待相機實拍驗證。請先用不重要的素材測試。

Install安裝

  1. Check the camera確認相機

    SIGMA fp (not fp L) on firmware Ver.5.02 exactly. How to check.

    SIGMA fp(不是 fp L),韌體必須剛好是 Ver.5.02。怎麼確認。

  2. Build the card in fpSup-Merge在 fpSup-Merge 做卡

    Open fpSup-Merge with fpSup-Gyro ticked. You can add OG3K or OG2K on the same card. You cannot add fpSup-Gyro-Base: the two hook the same places in the firmware. Then download the zip.

    打開已勾好 fpSup-Gyro 的 fpSup-Merge。 同一張卡可以再加 OG3K 或 OG2K。 不能跟 fpSup-Gyro-Base 一起用: 兩者掛在韌體裡同樣的位置。然後下載 zip。

    Fast Start 3 is an option on the same page. See How to use fpSup.

    Fast Start 3 是同一頁上的選項,見 fpSup 怎麼用。

  3. Copy to the card and start the camera複製到卡上並開機

    Unzip. Copy AutoRun.txt, fpSup.BIN and the FPSUPUI folder to the top level of the SD card the camera boots from. Do not put them in a folder. Details.

    解壓縮。把 AutoRun.txt、fpSup.BIN 和 FPSUPUI 資料夾複製到相機開機用那張 SD 卡的最上層。 不要放進資料夾裡。細節。

    Unplug the USB cable and turn the camera on. The fpSup logo appears in the top left and four boxes fill one by one. All four white = loaded. The camera's own screen comes back two seconds later. If the boxes stop filling, the load stopped there — see below.

    拔掉 USB 線,開機。左上角會出現 fpSup 標誌,四個方格一個一個填滿。 四格全白 = 載入完成。兩秒後相機原本的畫面會回來。 如果方格停住不再填,載入就是停在那裡 —— 見下面。

    There is no menu and nothing to turn on. No folder to make, nothing to convert.

    沒有選單,也沒有東西要開。不用建資料夾,不用轉換任何東西。

Before you shoot拍之前

you need你需要why為什麼
CinemaDNG recording用 CinemaDNG 錄影 A MOV take gets no files. MOV records through a different path this build does not hook. For MOV use fpSup-Gyro-Base.用 MOV 拍不會有這些檔案。 MOV 走的是另一條路徑,這個版本沒有掛在那裡。要錄 MOV 請用 fpSup-Gyro-Base。
A fast enough card夠快的卡 The files are written while you record, on the same card as the video. If the card is struggling, the gyro files are the first thing to go missing or come out incomplete. You still get the clip.這些檔案是在錄影的同時、寫在跟影片同一張卡上。 卡在吃力的時候,陀螺儀檔案會第一個不見或不完整。片子你還是有。
A lens with electronic contacts有電子接點的鏡頭 Only for the .json. The camera makes it from its own calibration data for the mounted lens. The .gcsv does not need it.只有 .json 需要。 相機是用它對掛上那顆鏡頭的校正資料做出來的。.gcsv 不需要。

Use使用

  1. Record CinemaDNG錄 CinemaDNG

    Record as usual. While you record, two files are written inside the clip's own folder:

    照平常錄。錄影的同時,片子自己的資料夾裡會寫出兩個檔案:

    \CINEMA\A001_037\A001_037.gcsvthe motion log運動記錄
    \CINEMA\A001_037\A001_037.jsonthe lens profile鏡頭 profile

    The log holds every sample the gyro produced, at 2499.466 Hz, nothing averaged. Each accelerometer reading sits on the row of the gyro sample it followed. The profile carries the mounted lens's own distortion — the same curve the camera puts in a DNG.

    記錄裡是陀螺儀產生的每一個樣本,2499.466 Hz,沒有平均。 每一筆加速度計讀數放在它之後那個陀螺儀樣本的那一列。 profile 帶的是掛上那顆鏡頭自己的畸變 —— 跟相機寫進 DNG 的是同一條曲線。

  2. Load into Gyroflow and sync載入 Gyroflow 並同步

    In Gyroflow, load the frames and both files, then run the sync.

    在 Gyroflow 裡載入影格和那兩個檔案,然後執行同步。

    You cannot skip the sync, and you cannot reuse a number from last time. CinemaDNG has no timecode, and the log starts about half a second after the first frame — between 470 and 570 ms, and different in every take.

    同步不能跳過,也不能沿用上次的數字。 CinemaDNG 沒有時間碼,而記錄的起點比第一個影格晚大約半秒 —— 落在 470 到 570 ms 之間,而且每一次拍攝都不一樣。

    Gyroflow asks for a frame rate when you give it a DNG sequence. Enter the rate you recorded at — it cannot read that from the frames.

    你把 DNG 序列丟給 Gyroflow 時,它會問你格率。 填你錄影時用的那個 —— 它沒辦法從影格裡讀出來。

    If you work in DaVinci Resolve如果你用 DaVinci Resolve

    There is a Gyroflow plugin for Resolve, so you can stabilise on the timeline instead of exporting from Gyroflow first. It reads the same two files.

    Resolve 有 Gyroflow 的外掛,所以你可以直接在時間軸上穩定, 不用先從 Gyroflow 匯出。它讀的是同樣那兩個檔案。

    If you use an adapter or a manual lens如果你用轉接環或手動鏡頭

    The camera may not know the lens, or may report the wrong one. The .json is then wrong, and Gyroflow corrects the picture for a lens you are not using. The .gcsv is not affected.

    相機可能不認得那顆鏡頭、或回報成錯的那一顆。 那樣 .json 就是錯的,Gyroflow 會照一顆你沒在用的鏡頭去修正畫面。 .gcsv 不受影響。

    What to do: do not use the .json. Use Gyroflow's own lens calibration: film a calibration target, and Gyroflow works out your lens's real distortion and saves a profile you can reuse for that lens.

    怎麼辦:不要用那個 .json,改用 Gyroflow 自己的鏡頭校正: 拍一張校正圖卡,Gyroflow 會算出你鏡頭真正的畸變,存成 profile,以後那顆鏡頭都能重複用。

    Portrait takes直拍

    Nothing to do. In CINE the camera records every frame landscape upright, so Gyroflow reads a portrait take like any other: load, sync, stabilise, and turn the picture at the end of the edit. Leave horizon lock off. Photographs are not touched and still rotate by themselves.

    不用做任何事。在 CINE 模式下,相機每一格都以橫向正立記錄, 所以 Gyroflow 讀直拍跟讀其他素材一樣:載入、同步、穩定,剪輯最後再把畫面轉過來。 水平鎖定維持關閉。照片不受影響,照樣會自己旋轉。

Remove移除

fpSup-Gyro has no menu setting, so there is nothing to set back first. Delete AutoRun.txt, fpSup.BIN and FPSUPUI from the card, or take the card out, then switch the camera off. At power-off the card writes back every firmware word it changed. Nothing is flashed.

fpSup-Gyro 沒有選單設定,所以不用先改回任何東西。 把卡上的 AutoRun.txt、fpSup.BIN 和 FPSUPUI 刪掉, 或把卡拿出來,然後關機。關機時卡會把它改過的每個韌體字寫回去。沒有任何東西被燒錄。

If the camera froze, unplug USB and take the battery out. More on removing.

如果相機凍住了,拔掉 USB、把電池拿出來。 移除的更多說明。

If you combined it with OG3K or OG2K, follow the removal steps for that product first — its resolution is a menu setting.

如果你跟 OG3K 或 OG2K 合在一起用,先照那個產品的移除步驟做 —— 它的解析度是選單設定。
